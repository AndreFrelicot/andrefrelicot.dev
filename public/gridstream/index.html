GridStream

Watch up to twelve videos at once on iPad, streamed from your Mac over your local network. No account, no cloud, no data collected.

1. The Mac server

GridStream Server scans your video folders (.mp4, .m4v, .mov) and streams them to the iPad. It lives in the menu bar.

Download for Mac

GridStream 1.0 · disk image signed and notarized by Apple · 23.6 MB · macOS 15.6 or later

2. The iPad app

Free on the App Store, for iPadOS 17 or later. It finds your Mac on the local network.

Getting started

  1. Open GridStream Server on your Mac and add one or more video folders.
  2. Open GridStream on the iPad, on the same network: it finds the Mac by itself.
  3. Enter the PIN shown in the server's menu bar menu.
